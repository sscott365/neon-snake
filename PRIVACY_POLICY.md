# Neon Snake Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 14, 2026**

This policy explains how Neon Snake (the “App”) handles information when you play. The developer can be contacted at **stevenscottfarms@gmail.com**.

## Information stored for the leaderboard

When a leaderboard result is submitted, the App stores a record in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore. The leaderboard record fields are:

- **Name:** the player name shown with the score (for example, a chosen display name).
- **Score:** the score achieved in the game.
- **UID:** a Firebase user identifier used to associate the record with a player. It is an identifier, not an email address.
- **Updated time:** the date and time the record was last updated.

The Firestore database is configured in the `us-central1` region. Google processes and stores this information to provide the database service, subject to Google’s terms and privacy documentation. The developer uses this information to operate and display the leaderboard and to maintain game records.

The listed leaderboard fields do not include an email address. This statement describes the leaderboard record; it does not mean other app components or third-party SDKs cannot process additional data.

## Advertising and third-party services

The App uses Google AdMob to serve ads. Google and its advertising partners may process device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions, and related information to deliver, measure, and protect advertising. What is processed can depend on your device settings, region, and ad personalization choices.

The App uses Google Firebase Cloud Firestore for leaderboard records. The App does not use Firebase Analytics or Firebase Authentication unless those services are separately added to the app; the UID in a record alone does not establish which sign-in method is used.

For details about Google’s data practices, see:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Firebase Privacy and Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- [AdMob Help and policies](https://support.google.com/admob/)

The developer does not sell leaderboard information. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or to protect the App and its users.

## Retention and security

Leaderboard records are retained while needed to operate the leaderboard and maintain game records, or as otherwise required for legitimate operational or legal purposes. The developer uses reasonable measures to protect information but cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Your choices and data requests

You can stop using the App by uninstalling it. Device settings may provide controls for advertising identifiers or ad personalization. To ask about or request deletion of a leaderboard record associated with you, email **stevenscottfarms@gmail.com**. Include your player name and, if available, your Firebase UID so the record can be located. Do not send passwords or other sensitive information. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

## Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact **stevenscottfarms@gmail.com** so the information can be reviewed and deleted where appropriate. If the intended audience or actual data practices change, this policy and the applicable Google Play Families declarations must be updated.

This policy may be updated as the App or its data practices change. The latest version and its updated date will be posted at the policy URL.
