Neon Snake Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy describes how Neon Snake (the “App”) handles information when you use it. The developer can be contacted at stevenscottfarms@gmail.com.

Information the App handles

  • Game and leaderboard data: When the App submits game results, it uses Google Firebase Cloud Firestore to store game-related records such as scores and leaderboard data. Records may be associated with an app-generated player identifier, display name, or submission time if those values are used by the current game implementation. The App does not require you to provide an email address to submit a score unless a future version adds an account feature.
  • Advertising data: The App uses Google AdMob to serve ads. Google and its advertising partners may process device and advertising identifiers, ad interactions, and related information to deliver, measure, and protect advertising. The data handled can depend on your device settings, region, and ad personalization choices.
  • Information stored on your device: The App may store game state, settings, or scores locally so the game can work.

The App’s use of Firebase Firestore does not by itself mean that Firebase Analytics or Firebase Authentication is used. This policy will be updated if the App adds those or other data-collecting services.

How information is used

Information is used to operate gameplay and leaderboards, deliver and measure ads, maintain service security, and troubleshoot or improve the App where applicable.

Service providers and sharing

The App uses Google Firebase Cloud Firestore to store game-related records and Google AdMob to provide advertising. These providers process information as described in their own privacy documentation and under their applicable terms. The developer does not sell personal information. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or to protect the App and its users.

  • Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Google AdMob policies and help

Storage, retention, and security

Firestore data is stored using Google Firebase. Game records are retained while needed to operate the game and its leaderboard, or as otherwise required for legitimate operational or legal purposes. The developer uses reasonable measures to protect information but cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and data requests

You can stop further collection by uninstalling the App. Device settings may provide controls for advertising identifiers or ad personalization. To ask about or request deletion of data associated with you, email stevenscottfarms@gmail.com and include enough detail to identify the relevant game record; do not send passwords or sensitive information. Some data may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact stevenscottfarms@gmail.com so the data can be reviewed and deleted where appropriate. If the intended audience or actual data practices change, this section must be revised and the applicable Google Play Families requirements followed.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the App or its data practices change. The latest version and its updated date will be posted at the policy URL.
